Rev C changes

Fixed wheels, lighter frame

  • Steering removed: both M0601 wheels are fixed and bolted to rigid 6 mm brackets (no suspension, shim slots). Turning is by wheel-speed difference.
  • Track 430 mm; the robot spins about its middle.
  • LiDAR in a front-centre slot at x = 350, ≈ 225° view; sides and rear closed.
  • Upper section removed: the deck sits directly on the LiDAR level. Height 305 → 245 mm.
  • Top frame, deck tube, mid tray, upper shell and steel walls replaced by 4 folded steel channels (top ring + walls in one, riveted). Deck now 3 mm Al.
  • Frame: 56 → 14 welded parts + 4 channels, ≈ 80 → 16 welds. Robot ≈ 46 → 27 kg.
